How I work

Curious about the detail. Focused on the outcome.

I've worked across healthcare SaaS, electronic health records, clinical data, health information exchange, higher-education platforms, and enterprise support environments. That variety taught me not to assume the first visible symptom is the source of the issue.

My best work tends to happen between teams: translating unclear behavior into testable facts, connecting technical findings to operational impact, and making sure what we learned doesn't disappear into one ticket.
